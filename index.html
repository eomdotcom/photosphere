    How to read the dashboard

    Zones

    Each student's concern score places them in one zone:

    • Calm Orbit or less
    • Steady Star above , up to
    • Solar Flare Zone over

    Near a zone line

    Students within points of or : a small change could move them into the next zone.

    List: marked ▲ (close to the zone above) or ▼ (below). Planets: their names sit beside the dashed line.

    Changed zone

    Students in a different zone from the week before. Not available for the first week.

    List: shows "was Calm Orbit" under the name. Planets: amber moved up a zone, teal moved down.

    Cases and notes

    Solar Flare Zone students get a case: New → Contacted → Meeting → Support plan → Closed.

    Click any student to open their page, update the case and add adviser notes. A zone is a prompt to look closer, not a decision.
